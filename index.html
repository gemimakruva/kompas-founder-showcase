How it works

1

Take the assessment

48 targeted questions about how you make decisions, handle risk, lead teams, and build. Takes about 15 minutes.

2

Claude analyzes your responses

Your answers are processed through our psychometric model. Claude interprets the patterns across 7 dimensions to determine your founder archetype and DNA profile.

3

Get your report

A personalized PDF covering your archetype, dimensional scores, DNA profile breakdown, and coaching recommendations written specifically for your pattern.
